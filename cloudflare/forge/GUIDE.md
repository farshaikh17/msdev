# Forge Websites: how to build and send a site

For Mianro's internal team. Sign in at https://forge-demo.dev1-024.workers.dev/login.

## 1. Build

1. In the box, type the business name or paste its Google Maps link.
2. Add the city if you know it (it gives the site a map). Add what they do if the name doesn't say.
3. Press **Build website**. It takes 10 to 20 minutes. You can close the page.

Worth adding under **More options** when you have it:
- **Phone.** A Google Maps link doesn't give us the phone number. Without one the site has no call button.
- **Their current website.** We read it for the phone, address, hours, services and photos.
- **Studio** for the premium tier, and a **look** if you want a particular one. Left alone, Forge picks one that suits the trade and differs from your last sites.

## 2. Look at it

Open the site's page. The banner at the top says where it is and what to do next.

- **Quality check** gives a score out of 10 and what it found. It runs by itself a few minutes after the build.
- **QA standards** shows the 13 rules our QA team uses (logo links, footer links, legal pages, dead buttons, headings, forms, phone). Faults are repaired automatically; anything still open is listed.
- Open the full site and look at it on your phone too.

## 3. Make it better

- **Fix everything** applies what the quality check found, in one press. No credit.
- **Request a change**: say what you want in your own words and pick the page.
- **Check again** re-runs the quality check and the QA standards after your changes.

## 4. Send it

- **Copy** the website link.
- The **Before & after page** puts their current site next to the new one. It is a strong first thing to send.
- The page shows when the link has been opened.

Under **More tools**: an email, text and call script written for the business, a QR code, a quick find-and-replace for a wrong phone number or name, and starting over with a new design.

## If something goes wrong

- **The build stopped.** Press **Try again**. It carries on from the pages already written and the credit was refunded. If it fails twice, send the message on the page to the administrator.
- **The site has the wrong trade or pages.** Type what they do in "What they do" and use "Start over with a new design".
- **A photo doesn't suit the business.** The pictures are AI previews until the business's own photos replace them. Add photo links under More options and redesign, or request a change on that page.
